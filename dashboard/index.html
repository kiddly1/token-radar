Each dot is a token that already trades: its TVL against its market cap. The line is the fitted curve. Big protocols trade at a smaller multiple of TVL than small ones — the curve bends down, so a $2B vault is not priced like ten $200M vaults.

Growth

Growth is the 7-day TVL change, clamped to −60%…+120% and given half weight: 1 + change × 0.5. A protocol shrinking fast scores 0.70; one growing 120% scores 1.60.

What counts as pre-token

The snapshot records no token when market cap, Gecko id and symbol are all empty. Wrapped, staked, bridged and exchange products are excluded — their receipt tokens already trade. We say “records no token”, never “has no token”: the snapshot is not proof.

Farm plan verdicts

Every plan is costed in dollars (6 transactions at the chain's typical gas, plus about $2 in swap and bridge fees) and netted against the low end of the value band at a 0.001% farmer share. FARM clears $50 net, THIN covers its cost, SKIP costs more than the low-end case is worth.
