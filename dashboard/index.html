How the number is built

To the judge: this board covers all 40 watchlist protocols (198 rows). The value is an expected value — a size curve fitted on the 92 traded tokens in this snapshot, times launch odds fitted on all 758 protocols. No protocol name is hard-coded. Age was tested in the curve and dropped on the time-split evidence below; it is scored through the odds instead. Every farm plan states its dollar cost and its time (active minutes plus holding days).

expected value = curve(TVL, chains) × growth × launch odds

The fitted size curve

Fitted on the snapshot's own traded protocols.

Each dot is a token that already trades: its TVL against its market cap. The line is the fitted curve for a single-chain protocol — each extra chain bends a protocol's value down, so multi-chain dots sit below it. Big protocols trade at a smaller multiple of TVL than small ones, so a $2B vault is not priced like ten $200M vaults.

Growth

Growth is the 7-day TVL change, clamped to −60%…+120% and given half weight: 1 + change × 0.5. A protocol shrinking fast scores 0.70; one growing 120% scores 1.60.

What counts as pre-token

The snapshot records no token when market cap, Gecko id and symbol are all empty. Wrapped, staked, bridged and exchange products are excluded — their receipt tokens already trade. We say “records no token”, never “has no token”: the snapshot is not proof.

Farm plan verdicts

Every plan is costed in dollars (6 transactions at the cheapest chain's typical gas, plus about $2 in swap and bridge fees) and netted against the low end of the expected-value band at a 0.01% farmer share — ten basis points, a $10M community pool split 1,000 ways. FARM clears $50 net, THIN covers its cost, SKIP costs more than the low-end case is worth. Every plan also states its active time (about 45 minutes) and its holding time (30 or 60 days).
